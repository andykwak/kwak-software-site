The short version.

PhonoOut

PhonoOut rips and plays your vinyl records. Your recordings and your collection stay on your computer. PhonoOut goes online only to look a record up, when you search for one.

  • Looking records up. When you search for a record while ripping, PhonoOut sends the text you type (such as the artist, the album title or a catalogue number) to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), once you pause typing. When you pick a result, it asks MusicBrainz for that release's details and downloads its front and back covers from the Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org). Like any web request, these show your IP address, and they name the app and its version, as MusicBrainz asks. Nothing else about you or your collection is sent. MusicBrainz is run by the MetaBrainz Foundation; see its privacy policy. If you'd rather not look a record up, you can type in or paste its details yourself, and nothing is sent.
  • No other network access. PhonoOut makes no other internet connections. It has no analytics, crash reporting or update checks, and no account.
  • Recording. PhonoOut records from the sound input you choose (for example a turntable connected through a sound card or USB), and only while you are ripping a side.
  • Your collection. Your rips are saved in a folder in your Music folder. PhonoOut keeps its library (each record's details, tracks and artwork, and where the details came from) in a folder in your user folder, along with takes you haven't saved yet. Display choices such as the view, theme and zoom are kept by the app on your computer too. It all stays on your computer.

When you contact us

If you email us or use the contact form on our website, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to help with your question. We keep messages for as long as we need them to help you and to keep ordinary business records, and we delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy, which handle it on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

Where information is stored

MetaBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (which answer PhonoOut's lookups) and FormSubmit (which delivers messages from our contact form) may store or process information outside Canada, for example in the United States. Information held there may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country under its laws.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@kwaksoftware.ca and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

PhonoOut is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
