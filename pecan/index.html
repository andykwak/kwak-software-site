Good to know

  • Pecan organizes photos on your computer. It doesn't sync with phones or online photo services.
  • Its library and thumbnails are kept in your Windows user folder, so the first look through a large collection takes a little while.
  • Pecan changes a photo file only when you ask: saving a rotation into a JPEG, writing a new date, renaming, or moving a duplicate to the Recycle Bin.
Questions

Before you try it

What happens when the trial ends?

Pecan keeps opening and you can still browse your timeline. Everything else asks for a licence key. Nothing is charged, and your library, albums and fixes are kept for when you buy.

Does Pecan upload my photos?

No. It makes no internet connections, has no account and collects nothing about you.

Will it change or move my files?

No, not unless you ask it to rename them, change their date, save a rotation into them or move duplicates to the Recycle Bin.

Is it Picasa?

No. It's a new app by a small Canadian company, made for people who miss how quick and simple Picasa was.

Can I buy it from the Microsoft Store instead?

It's coming to the Microsoft Store too, with the same 14-day trial and price. A Store purchase stays in the Store, and a purchase here comes with a licence key.
