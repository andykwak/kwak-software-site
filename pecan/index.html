Pecan

In development

A fast photo organizer for the photos and videos on your computer. Everything it does happens on your computer.

Tell me when it's available
What it does

Your photo library, on your own computer

  • Shows the photos and videos in the folders you choose on one timeline
  • Albums and favourites, and finds exact duplicates so you can clear them out
  • Fixes photos without touching the originals: crop, straighten, colour and auto-enhance
  • Exports originals or smaller copies, with the option to remove location and personal details
  • Leaves your files where they are, and keeps rotations and albums when you move or rename them
