Pecan privacy

Effective November 22, 2026.

Pecan isn't available yet. This page describes the app as it is being built; we'll check it again, and update it if anything changed, before Pecan is released.

The short version.

  • Pecan makes no internet connections of its own. It has no analytics, ads or account.
  • Your library and thumbnails stay in a folder on your computer.
  • Pecan changes your files only when you ask it to.

This page covers Pecan; our privacy policy covers our website and our other apps. The owner of Kwak Software is responsible for how personal information is handled and can be reached at support@kwaksoftware.ca.

Pecan

Pecan organizes the photos and videos on your computer. Everything it does happens on your computer.

  • No network access. Pecan makes no internet connections of its own. It has no analytics, crash reporting or update checks, and no account. It opens our website or a new email in your email app only if you click those links in About, Settings or the Help menu.
  • Your licence. Pecan asks Windows for its Microsoft Store licence (a trial, or bought), which Windows keeps on your computer, so Pecan works without the internet. If you choose Buy Pecan…, the Microsoft Store's own purchase window opens; that purchase is between you and Microsoft (see below).
  • Your library. Pecan reads the folders you choose. It keeps its library (file names and locations, dates, camera details, location data stored in your photos, albums, favourites, hidden photos and edits) and thumbnails in a folder in your user folder. They stay on your computer, and deleting that folder removes them.
  • Files stored online. Pecan doesn't open files that are kept only online (for example OneDrive files that are "online-only"), so it never makes them download. For their thumbnails it asks Windows, which gets a small picture from your cloud storage app under that service's own privacy policy.
  • Your files. Pecan changes a file only when you ask it to: when you rename photos (their Live Photo videos and sidecar files are renamed with them), write a new date into files after adjusting it, turn on saving rotations into JPEG files, or delete photos or duplicates (they go to the Recycle Bin). Collages are saved as new files in a Pecan Collages folder in your Pictures folder. Fixes such as crops and colour leave the original files as they are.
  • Exports. When you export photos, you can choose to remove location, camera and other personal details from the copies.
  • Crash reports and logs. If Pecan crashes, it writes a plain-text report to a logs folder beside its library, with your Windows user name taken out of file paths, and mentions it the next time it starts. It also notes problems there, such as a video that won't play or the window not responding, which can include file names. None of this is ever sent. If you choose to email a report to us, we use it only to fix the problem.

Buying on our website

If you buy Pecan on our website, Paddle.com, our reseller, handles the order and payment. We receive your name, email address, country, what you bought and the order number, to send your licence key and help you. Your key contains your name and order number; Pecan checks it on your computer and never sends it anywhere. We never send marketing email. Our privacy policy has the details, including the services that help create and email your key.

Buying and installing through the Microsoft Store

If you get Pecan from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase, payment and download under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We never see your payment details. Microsoft gives us reports such as sales counts by country, ratings and reviews you post publicly in the Store, and, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings, crash reports. We use these only to support and improve Pecan.

When you contact us

If you email us or use the contact form on our website, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to help with your question. We keep messages for as long as we need them to help you and to keep ordinary business records, and we delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy, which handle it on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

Where information is stored

Paddle, Cloudflare and Resend (if you buy Pecan on our website), Microsoft (if you get the app through the Microsoft Store) and FormSubmit (which delivers messages from our contact form) may store or process information outside Canada, for example in the United States. Information held there may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country under its laws.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@kwaksoftware.ca and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Pecan is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.
