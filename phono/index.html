Phono

In development

Digitizes and plays your vinyl collection. Rip a record, and Phono finds the album, its tracks and its artwork. Then flip through your collection the way you would in a record store.

Tell me when it's available
What it does

Your records, ripped and on the shelf

  • Rips each side of a record, all at once or one side at a time
  • Looks the album up on MusicBrainz, or lets you enter it yourself, and splits each side into tracks
  • Keeps the front and back covers, labels, lyric sheets and other artwork with each record
  • Browse your collection as a crate you flip through, pull a record out and turn it over
  • Plays a side on a spinning turntable: drag the tonearm or click a band of grooves to jump to a track
