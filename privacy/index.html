Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026.

The short version.

  • Kwak Software apps run on your computer. They don't collect analytics, don't show ads and don't need an account.
  • Your photos, documents, recordings and other files stay on your computer. Most of our apps never connect to the internet; PhonoOut goes online only when you look a record up.
  • We only get personal information when you choose to send it to us, for example by email or the contact form, or when you buy an app here. We use it only to reply to you, deliver what you bought and keep the records the law requires.
  • We never send marketing email.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Who we are

Kwak Software is a sole proprietorship based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Kwak Software. This policy covers our apps and this website (kwaksoftware.ca); each app's own page, listed below, gives the details for that app. The owner of Kwak Software is responsible for how personal information is handled and can be reached at support@kwaksoftware.ca.

Our apps

Each app has its own privacy page that says exactly what it does with your information. In short:

  • ReKarver privacy
    Recovers photos and videos from drives and memory cards. Makes no internet connections.
  • ReKarver Documents privacy
    Recovers deleted documents. Makes no internet connections.
  • Pecan privacy
    Organizes the photos and videos on your computer. Makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Purrsor privacy
    Puts a cat toy on your mouse pointer. Makes no internet connections.
  • PhonoOut privacy
    Rips and plays your vinyl records. Goes online only when you look a record up on MusicBrainz.

Buying on this website

ReKarver and Pecan bought on this website are sold by Paddle.com, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle collects your name, email address, country and postal code (for sales tax), and your payment details, under the Paddle Privacy Notice. We never see your card or PayPal details. Paddle gives us your name, email address, country, what you bought and the order number. We use them to make and send your licence key, to answer support and refund requests, and to keep the business records the law requires (in Canada, six years).

To make and deliver your key, your name, email address and licence key also pass through Cloudflare, which runs the small program that creates the key (Cloudflare Privacy Policy), and Resend, which sends the email with your key (Resend Privacy Policy). They use it only to do that for us.

Your licence key contains your name and order number. The apps check it on your computer and never send it anywhere. We never send marketing email.

Buying and installing through the Microsoft Store

If you buy one of our apps through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase, payment and download under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We never see your payment details. Microsoft gives us reports such as sales counts by country, ratings and reviews you post publicly in the Store, and, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings, crash reports. We use these only to support and improve our apps.

This website

  • No cookies or tracking. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, and may log them for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Contact form. When you use the contact form, the name, email address, topic and message you enter are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers them to our inbox.

When you contact us

If you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to help with your question. We keep messages for as long as we need them to help you and to keep ordinary business records, and we delete them when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above, which handle it on our behalf (for example to deliver email), or when the law requires it.

Where information is stored

Some of the service providers named in this policy, including Paddle, Cloudflare, Resend, Microsoft, GitHub and FormSubmit, may store or process information outside Canada, for example in the United States. Information held there may be accessed by courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country under its laws.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to support@kwaksoftware.ca and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Our apps and website are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If an app starts collecting something new, we'll say so here before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@kwaksoftware.ca or use the contact form.
