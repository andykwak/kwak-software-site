Privacy

Privacy policy

Kwak Software apps are meant to run on your computer and leave your personal data alone.

  • ReKarver does not collect analytics or usage data, and does not need an account.
  • Photos ReKarver recovers are saved only to a folder you choose on your computer.
  • If you contact us through this site, your name, email and message are used only to reply to you. Messages from the form are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
