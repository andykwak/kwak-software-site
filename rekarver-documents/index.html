ReKarver Documents

In development

ReKarver's sister app, for documents instead of photos. It brings back deleted documents from a drive, memory card or USB stick, with their original names and dates. Like ReKarver, it only reads from the drive and never writes to it.

Tell me when it's available
What it does

Get your documents back

  • Finds deleted documents that the drive still keeps a record of
  • Brings them back under their own names and dates
  • Shows what it found as a list of names, with a preview, so you can pick what to keep
  • Saves the documents it recovers to a folder you choose
Document types
PDF · Word · Excel · PowerPoint · OpenDocument · RTF · text · CSV · ZIP
Drives
FAT · exFAT · NTFS
Account needed
No
