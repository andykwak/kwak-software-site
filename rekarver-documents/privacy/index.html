The short version.

  • ReKarver Documents makes no internet connections. It has no analytics, ads or account.

ReKarver Documents

ReKarver Documents recovers deleted documents from drives, memory cards and USB sticks. It is built on the same engine as ReKarver and works the same way: everything happens on your computer.

  • No network access. ReKarver Documents makes no internet connections. It has no analytics, crash reporting or update checks of its own, and no account. It opens our website, this page or a new email in your email app only if you click those links.
  • Your drives and documents. It reads the drive or image you choose. It doesn't change it. Documents it recovers are saved only to the folder you choose, and never onto the drive being scanned unless you choose that after a warning.
  • Previews. When you select a document, its preview is read from the drive on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to show it.
  • While a scan runs. What it finds (the documents' names, folders, dates and sizes as the drive lists them, and where they are on the drive) is kept in a working folder in your computer's temporary folder. If you don't save the scan, that folder is deleted when you go back, scan again or close the app.
  • Scans you save. If you save a scan, it is written to one file where you choose. It holds the list of what was found, including the documents' names and folders, so keep it as carefully as the documents themselves.
  • Settings, crash log and diagnostics. As in ReKarver: your settings are kept in a small file in your user folder, an error the app can't recover from is written to a crash.log file there, and Copy diagnostics copies details to your clipboard only. Nothing is sent.
