Help and support

If your photos matter, stop using the drive now. Don't take new photos on the card, copy files to the drive or format it. Anything written to it can land on top of the photos you want back. Then scan it with ReKarver and save the photos to a different drive.

Contact us

Email support@kwaksoftware.ca or use the contact form. In ReKarver, Help › Contact support (F1) starts an email with the version filled in. We answer within two business days, in English or French.

What to include

  1. What you were recovering from (SD card, USB stick, hard drive) and roughly how big it is.
  2. What happened to the photos: deleted, card formatted, card won't open.
  3. Which scan you ran and what you saw.
  4. Diagnostics. In ReKarver, choose Help › Copy diagnostics (Ctrl+Shift+D) and paste it into your email. It lists your Windows version, ReKarver's version, the drives it sees and its last few hundred steps. It doesn't include your photos or their names, and nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Questions and answers

Can ReKarver damage my drive or make things worse?

No. ReKarver only reads the drive; it never writes to it. It also keeps its working files and the photos you save off that drive. If it is the only drive in the computer, ReKarver warns you and asks before writing anything to it.

What can it recover from?

SD and microSD cards, USB sticks, external and internal hard drives, and disk image files (.img, .dd, .raw, .001 and similar). It reads drives formatted FAT, FAT32, exFAT and NTFS best, with names and dates. Other drives, such as ones from a Mac or Linux computer, are still searched, but photos come back without their original names.

Which kinds of files does it find?

Photos: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, BMP, TIFF and camera raw (DNG, Canon CR2 and CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Pentax PEF). Videos: MP4, MOV and 3GP. It doesn't recover documents or music.

Why does Windows ask for permission when I press Scan?

Reading a drive directly needs administrator rights in Windows. ReKarver asks only at that moment, and only to read. Disk image files don't need it.

Which scan should I use?

Start with Quick scan. It finds deleted files the drive still lists, usually in seconds, often with their names. If what you want isn't there, or the drive was formatted, use Scan free space: it searches the empty part of the drive, where deleted files are. Scan whole drive reads everything and is the slowest.

How long does a scan take?

A quick scan takes seconds to a few minutes. Free-space and whole-drive scans go about as fast as the drive can be read: roughly 15 to 20 minutes for a 64 GB USB 3 stick, and much longer for a large hard drive or through a slow card reader.

The card was formatted. Can I still get photos back?

Usually, after a quick format, which is what cameras and Windows do by default. Use Scan free space or Scan whole drive. A full format writes zeros over the whole drive, and then nothing can be recovered.

ReKarver found nothing. Why?
  • It is an SSD. Most SSDs erase deleted data on their own soon after a file is deleted.
  • The space was used again by new files.
  • The drive is encrypted with BitLocker. ReKarver can't search encrypted drives.
  • Only a quick scan was run. Try Scan free space.
Some photos are marked Partial, or are grey at the bottom. Why?

Part of the file was overwritten or is missing. ReKarver saves what is left, and the missing part shows grey. That is usually as much of that photo as still exists.

Why are some photos listed twice, or why do I see lots of tiny pictures?

Drives often hold several copies of the same photo, and programs store small icons and thumbnails. In the View menu, "Show each picture once" and "Hide very small pictures" tidy the list.

Can I stop now and save more photos later?

Yes. File › Save Scan… keeps what the scan found in one .rekarve file. Open it later, or double-click it, and save more photos without scanning again. Keep the same drive connected and unchanged; photos that have changed on it since the scan won't be saved.

Can ReKarver play recovered videos?

No. It lists them with their first frame and saves them. Open them afterwards in any video player.

Does ReKarver send my photos anywhere?

No. ReKarver doesn't connect to the internet at all. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Before you buy

  • Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11, on a 64-bit (x64) PC.
  • Administrator permission to read a drive. Disk image files don't need it.
  • ReKarver can't recover data that has been overwritten, erased by an SSD, or encrypted with BitLocker.
  • ReKarver is sold through the Microsoft Store, so purchases and refunds go through Microsoft.
