Privacy

ReKarver privacy

Effective November 22, 2026.

The short version.

  • ReKarver makes no internet connections. It has no analytics, ads or account.
  • It only reads the drive you choose and never changes it. Recovered photos go only to the folder you choose.
  • What it keeps (settings, a crash log, scans you save) stays on your computer.

ReKarver is made by Kwak Software, a sole proprietorship based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Kwak Software. This page covers ReKarver; our privacy policy covers our website and our other apps. The owner of Kwak Software is responsible for how personal information is handled and can be reached at support@kwaksoftware.ca.

ReKarver

ReKarver recovers photos and videos from drives, memory cards and disk images. Everything it does happens on your computer.

  • No network access. ReKarver makes no internet connections. It has no analytics, crash reporting or update checks of its own, and no account. It opens our website, this page or a new email in your email app only if you click those links in About or the Help menu.
  • Your drives and photos. ReKarver reads the drive or image you choose. It doesn't change it. Photos it recovers are saved only to the folder you choose, and never onto the drive being scanned unless you choose that after a warning.
  • While a scan runs. ReKarver keeps what it finds (a list of the photos, where they are on the drive and small previews of them) in a working folder in your computer's temporary folder. If you don't save the scan, that folder is deleted when you go back, scan again or close ReKarver.
  • Scans you save. If you choose File › Save Scan…, ReKarver writes one scan file (.rekarve) where you choose. It holds the list of what was found, with the names, dates and other details the drive and the photos record, small previews of the photos, the drive's name and size, and a log of the scan. Anyone who can open the file can see those previews, so keep it as carefully as the photos themselves.
  • Settings. ReKarver keeps your language and how you like photos shown and saved in a small settings file in your user folder. It stays on your computer.
  • Crash log. If ReKarver runs into an error it can't recover from, it writes what happened to a crash.log file in the same folder and tells you where it is. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Diagnostics. If you choose Copy diagnostics, ReKarver copies details about your computer and what the app did (not the names of the photos it found) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent. If you paste it into an email to us, we use it only to help with your problem.
