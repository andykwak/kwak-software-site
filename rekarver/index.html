What it does

Deleted photos and videos

Finds photos and videos that were deleted, or that are on a card or drive that won't open or asks to be formatted.

Names and dates come back

On FAT, exFAT and NTFS drives it reads the deleted file entries too, so many files return with their names and dates, and NTFS files that were in pieces come back whole.

What it can't do

  • Solid-state drives (SSDs) usually erase deleted files by themselves within minutes, so there is often nothing left to find on them.
  • A full format, which writes zeros over the drive, leaves nothing to recover. A quick format usually does.
  • Files that were in pieces come back whole only on NTFS. Elsewhere a file in pieces is cut short or skipped.
  • Mac and Linux drives (APFS, HFS+, ext4) are searched by content only, so files come back without their names.
  • Olympus, Panasonic and Fujifilm raw files aren't recognised yet.
Questions

Before you buy

Is there a free trial?

No. ReKarver is a plain paid app at $9.99 CAD. If it doesn't help, ask for a refund within 30 days.

Should I keep using the card or drive?

No. Stop using it until you've recovered what you need. Every new photo or file written to it can overwrite the ones you're trying to get back.

Why does it need administrator permission?

Windows only lets programs read a whole drive, including its deleted parts, with administrator permission. ReKarver asks for it only for reading, and the app itself runs without it.

Does it need the internet?

No. Not to scan, not to save, and not to check your licence key.

Can I buy it from the Microsoft Store instead?

It's coming to the Microsoft Store too, at the same price. A Store purchase stays in the Store, and a purchase here comes with a licence key.

Can it recover from my phone?

It can read a phone's memory card in a card reader. It can't read a phone's built-in storage.
