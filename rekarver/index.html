ReKarver

Get your photos back from a memory card, USB stick or hard drive.

Deleted a folder by mistake, formatted the wrong card, or have a card your camera says is damaged? ReKarver looks through the drive for photos and videos, shows you what it found, and saves the ones you pick to your computer. It only ever reads the drive.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing is written to the drive

  1. Choose the drive

    Plug in the memory card, USB stick or drive and pick it from the list. A quick scan takes a few minutes. If that isn't enough, a deeper scan reads the drive's free space, or every byte of it.

  2. Look through what it finds

    Photos appear as they're found. Open any of them full size, see when it was taken and with which camera, and hide duplicates and tiny pictures.

  3. Save the ones you want

    Tick the photos you want and press Save selected. They go to a folder you choose on another drive, never back onto the drive being scanned.

Why ReKarver

Careful with the drive, honest about what it finds

Read-only, always

ReKarver opens the drive only to read it. Writing anything to a drive with deleted photos on it can overwrite them for good, so it never does.

Finds photos after a format

It recognizes photos and videos by their contents, not by the drive's list of files, so it can find them after the card was formatted or the list was damaged.

Keeps names and dates when it can

On FAT, exFAT and NTFS drives it reads the deleted entries the drive still lists, so many photos come back with their original names, folders and dates.

Checks each file

Every photo is checked to be whole before it's shown. One that was partly overwritten is marked as partial, so you know what you're getting.

Copes with failing cards

Sectors that can't be read are tried again and then skipped, so one bad spot doesn't stop the scan. If the drive is unplugged, it says so and keeps what it found.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. ReKarver doesn't connect to the internet, and your photos never leave your computer. See the privacy policy.
