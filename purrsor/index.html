Purrsor

In development

Puts a cat toy on the end of your mouse pointer, so your cat has something to chase and pounce on. Your mouse keeps working normally.

Tell me when it's available
What it does

Something for your cat to chase

  • Five toys: a feather wand, a toy mouse on a string, a rainbow ribbon, a laser dot and a buzzing bee
  • The toy follows your pointer, and clicks go straight through it to whatever is underneath
  • Realistic or cartoon toys, and a choice of how lively they move
  • Lives in the system tray, with keyboard shortcuts to hide the toy or switch to the next one
